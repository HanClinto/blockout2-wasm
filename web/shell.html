How to play

Blocks fall into a pit you look down into. Fill a whole layer and it disappears; the game ends when the pit fills up. Clearing several layers at once, and emptying the pit, scores the most.

← ↑ → ↓Move the block
Q W ETurn it around each axis (A S D the other way); Options › Controls changes them
SpaceDrop it
PPause (the game also pauses when you switch tabs)
EscLeave the game, or go back in the menus
F1Full screen

Choose Setup picks the pit: the classic Flat Fun, 3D Mania and Out of Control, or any size you like. Each pit has its own high scores; Online Score shows them (← → switch boards).
